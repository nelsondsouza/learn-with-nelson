# T02 Exercise — Paths Practice

Use this fictional tree:

```text
project/
├── README.md
├── data/
│   ├── raw/
│   │   └── sales.csv
│   └── processed/
│       └── summary.csv
├── docs/
│   └── guide.md
└── src/
    └── app.py
```

Use forward slashes for these conceptual relative paths.

## From `project/`

Write paths to `sales.csv`, `summary.csv`, `guide.md`, and `app.py`.

## From `docs/`

Write paths to `README.md`, `sales.csv`, and `app.py`.

## From `data/raw/`

Write paths to `summary.csv`, `README.md`, and `guide.md`.

Then explain absolute path, relative path, `.`, `..`, and why someone else's absolute path may fail on your computer.
