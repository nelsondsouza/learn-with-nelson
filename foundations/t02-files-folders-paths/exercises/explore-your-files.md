# T02 Exercise — Explore Your Files

Use your normal file manager. Do not delete or move important files.

## Inspect a safe file

Record its filename, extension, parent folder, file size, and full location/path.

## Create a practice area

Create:

```text
t02-practice/
├── documents/
│   └── notes.txt
├── data/
└── images/
```

Check whether your file manager displays `.txt`.

For `notes.txt`, identify its parent, its parent's parent, and the sibling folders of `documents`.

Explain in your own words why a folder hierarchy is useful.
