# T04 — First Workspace Example

```text
t04-first-workspace/
├── README.md
├── notes.txt
├── data/
│   └── sample.txt
├── docs/
│   └── vscode-notes.md
└── src/
```

The goal is to be comfortable opening a folder as a workspace, navigating it, creating/editing/renaming files, saving them, and finding them quickly.
