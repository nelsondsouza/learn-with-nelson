# T08 Exercise — Filtering and Sorting

Write queries to find:

1. students aged 24 or older
2. students with score above 90
3. students from Dubai
4. students from Dubai OR Doha
5. students aged at least 22 AND score at least 90
6. all students ordered by score highest first
7. all students ordered by age lowest first
8. top three scores

Explain the difference between `AND` and `OR`.
