# T08 Mini Challenge — Student Results Explorer

Using the `students` table, create `sql/challenge.sql`.

Write queries that answer:

1. Which three students have the highest scores?
2. Which students are from Kuwait City?
3. Which students are younger than 24?
4. Which students have missing email addresses?
5. What cities appear in the data without duplicates?
6. What is the average score?
7. What is the highest score?
8. How many students are there?
9. Show `name`, `age`, and age next year as `next_age`.
10. Show `name`, `score`, and a score increased by 2 as `adjusted_score`.

Requirements:

- use `SELECT`
- use `WHERE`
- use `ORDER BY`
- use `LIMIT`
- use `DISTINCT`
- use `AS`
- use at least one `AND` or `OR`
- use `IS NULL` or `IS NOT NULL`
- use at least three aggregate functions

Then commit and push `challenge.sql`.

Do not use joins, `GROUP BY`, subqueries, or window functions yet.
